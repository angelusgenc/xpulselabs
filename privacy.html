Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2025  ·  Applies to IG Pulse Pro & X Pulse Pro

📋Overview

XPulse Labs ("we", "our", "us") operates two desktop automation products: IG Pulse Pro and X Pulse Pro. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Key principle: Our apps work through your existing browser session. We never ask for, store, or transmit your social media passwords or login credentials — ever.

📦Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate our licensing and subscription service:

  • License & Account Data: Your email address and license key, collected when you purchase a subscription through Gumroad. Used solely to validate your subscription and deliver product access.
  • Usage Metadata: Basic app version, operating system type, and license validation timestamps — used to maintain service integrity and deliver updates.
  • Payment Information: Processed entirely by Gumroad. XPulse Labs does not collect, store, or handle credit card or payment details directly.
  • Support Communications: Any messages you send to our support email are stored for the purpose of resolving your request.

🚫What We Do NOT Collect

We are explicit about what our apps do not collect:

  • Your Instagram or X (Twitter) username, password, or session tokens
  • Your social media account content, posts, messages, or media
  • Any information transmitted through the browser session used for automation
  • Your IP address or precise geolocation data
  • Behavioral data or analytics from inside the app
  • Any data from your local file system beyond files you explicitly select for posting

🌐How the Apps Work — Browser Sessions

Both IG Pulse Pro and X Pulse Pro connect to your already-logged-in browser (e.g., Chrome) to automate posting actions. This is a standard desktop automation approach.

The automation occurs entirely on your local machine, using your own browser session, your own IP address, and your own network. No data from your social media session is transmitted to XPulse Labs servers.

Your credentials stay with you. The apps interact with the browser UI on your behalf — they do not access, read, or store your social media login credentials at any point.

🎨AI Studio Credits (IG Pulse Pro)

IG Pulse Pro's AI Studio feature uses a credit system for image and video generation. When you use AI Studio:

  • Your prompts or reference images are sent to a third-party AI generation service to produce content.
  • We do not permanently store your prompts or generated media beyond what is needed to deliver the result to your app.
  • Credit balances are tracked server-side and associated with your license key.

🔗Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Gumroad — Payment processing and license management. Your purchase is governed by Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • Email Provider — For support communications only.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

🔒Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect the limited data we hold. License validation uses encrypted communications. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you have security concerns, contact us at support@xpulselabs.com.

👤Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@xpulselabs.com and we will respond promptly.

🍪Cookies

Our desktop applications do not use cookies. Our website (xpulselabs.com) may use essential cookies for basic functionality only. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

📝Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notification or by email to active subscribers. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
